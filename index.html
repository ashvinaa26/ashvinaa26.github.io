About me

From finance to people.

I’m studying for an MSc in People Analytics and Human-Centric Management at UCL, after four years working at Baker Hughes, ABB and Prudential.

My background is in finance. I joined ABB’s Global Early Talent Programme, rotating through project controlling, project management and financial planning, then spent two years as a Sustainability Reporting Analyst before joining Prudential as Assistant Manager, Sustainability.

Through every role, the part I enjoyed most was the people side: partnering with HR on projects, and, as Lead University Ambassador at Baker Hughes, bringing students into the company. So I’m now moving into HR, focusing on organisational development and talent development.

I bring a business and finance view to people questions, and the analytics to back decisions with evidence.
