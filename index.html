The Big Mac index: which currencies look over- or undervalued?

Question: how much more or less does a Big Mac cost around the world than in America, at market exchange rates and after allowing for income?

Hover a row for its prices and rank. Click a row to pin its history from 2000 to 2026. Press Play to watch the ranking change over time. New in my version: once a country is pinned, add a second one (the box marked NEW, or Shift+click a row) to compare the two histories side by side.

What to notice: in July 2026 Switzerland is the dearest (+45% against the US dollar) and Indonesia the cheapest (−62%), and adjusting for income barely changes the typical gap (27% raw, 25% adjusted).
