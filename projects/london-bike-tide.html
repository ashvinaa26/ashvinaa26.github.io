London’s commuter tide

Every weekday, bikes flow into the City of London in the morning and back out in the evening. This animated map shows all 787 Santander Cycles docking stations hour by hour, from 06:00 to 22:00, coloured by whether more bikes arrive or leave.

  • DataTfL cycle hire usage data, August 2023
  • Coverage787 stations, 22 weekdays, 17 hours
  • ToolsPython, pandas, Plotly
  • ContextUCL Data Visualisation module

Open the map full screenStatic picture at 08:00 (PNG)

What to notice

Waterloo is where the morning commute starts. At 08:00 about 70 bikes leave Waterloo Station 3 in the hour and only 2 arrive, the biggest outflow of any station. Commuters off the trains ride over the river, and the City and the West End turn purple: Queen Street 1 by Bank gains 33 bikes in the hour.

The tide turns in the evening. At 17:00 the same Waterloo station gains 83 bikes in the hour, while stations around Bank and St Paul’s empty out.

How to use it

  • Press ▶ to play the day, or drag the slider to one hour.
  • Purple stations gain bikes (more arrive than leave); orange stations lose them. The colour stops at ±10, so the darkest dots may be far beyond it.
  • Hover a dot to see the station’s arrivals, departures and net change for that hour.

What I added: when is the network busiest?

The map shows where bikes go, but it is hard to compare hours by eye. This line adds up every station’s pick-ups and returns for each hour. The evening rush is bigger than the morning one: about 6,200 at 18:00 against about 5,400 at 08:00.
