Is your city getting warmer?

Each city is compared only with itself: how much warmer or cooler each year was than that city’s own 1961–1990 average, for 40 cities from 1950 to 2025.

  • DataOpen-Meteo.com (ERA5)
  • Coverage40 cities, 1950–2025
  • ToolsPython, pandas, Plotly, matplotlib
  • ContextMSIN0167 class exercise, UCL

Open the chart full screenStatic picture (PNG)

What to notice

Over the last ten years (2016–2025) London was 1.6 °C warmer than its own 1961–1990 average and Sydney 1.0 °C warmer. In 2025, Moscow was furthest above its own average of the 40 cities, at +3.3 °C. Warming is not even: Delhi is only about 0.3 °C above its own average over 2016–2025.

How to use it

  • Find a city to add up to four cities, or press one of the suggestions.
  • Show switches between the whole year, July and January (in the south, July is winter).
  • Play or the slider shows the top 10 cities for each year from 1950 to 2025.
New in my version: the 10-year average switch draws a thick line for each chosen city: the average of the ten years up to each year. Single years jump up and down; the smooth line shows the long-run trend more clearly.
