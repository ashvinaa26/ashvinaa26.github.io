      Source: Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), Historical Weather API, ERA5 reanalysis: generated using Copernicus Climate Change Service information, ECMWF. Daily mean air temperature at 2 m, averaged by month; 40 cities, 1950 to 2025. Changes made: monthly means and differences from the 1961–1990 average computed for this class exercise.

      “Its own 1961–1990 average” means that city’s average temperature for the same calendar month in 1961–1990, so each city is compared only with itself. The whole-year figure is the average of the twelve monthly differences. ERA5 is a computer reconstruction of past weather on a grid of about 25 km, not a thermometer at the city centre. July and January are the warm and cool months in the north; it is the other way round in the south. Rank 1 = warmest compared with its own average, among the 40 cities. Not endorsed by Open-Meteo, Copernicus or ECMWF.

      New in this version: the 10-year average switch draws each city's average of the ten years up to each year, so the long-run trend shows through the year-to-year ups and downs.
